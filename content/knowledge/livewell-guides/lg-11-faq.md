# LiveWell Coach: Frequently Asked Questions

LiveWell Coach is a workshop sample agent that gives general healthy-living information grounded in these guides. It is designed to be helpful for everyday choices, while staying clear about its limits.

## What can the coach help with?

The coach can explain healthy plate ideas, physical activity targets, Nutri-Grade, lower-salt and lower-sugar choices, sleep routines, screening follow-up, hazy-day activity, and staying active after 60. It can use your own workshop profile to make examples more relevant, such as your region, activity interests, and stated goals.

| Question type | Coach can help? | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General healthy-living guidance | Yes | "How can I make hawker meals healthier?" |
| Personal workshop profile | Yes, for your own profile only | "Suggest activities near my region." |
| Diagnosis or treatment | No | "What illness do I have?" |
| Urgent symptoms | No | "I have severe chest pain." |

## What are the limits?

LiveWell Coach is not a doctor. It does not diagnose, confirm a disease, replace clinic advice, or read real personal health records. It never advises on medication or doses — speak to your doctor or pharmacist.

If symptoms are urgent, call 995. Examples include severe chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, sudden weakness, or any situation where you think emergency help is needed.

For personal health records, use HealthHub. The workshop coach does not retrieve official records or update them.

## How does the coach use my profile?

The coach only uses your own profile and will never show another resident's information. In the workshop, the profile is sample data created for learning. It helps the coach personalise examples, such as suggesting indoor activity when your region is hazy or choosing food tips that match your stated goal.

The coach should not reveal hidden identifiers or compare you with another named resident. If a question asks for someone else's details, it should refuse and explain that it can only use your own profile.

## What are Healthpoints and eVouchers?

Healthpoints are the Healthy 365 rewards currency. Residents can earn Healthpoints through eligible activities in the Healthy 365 ecosystem.

eVouchers are merchant vouchers that residents redeem with Healthpoints. The coach can explain these terms in general workshop language, but it does not manage real rewards balances.

## How does activity sign-up work?

The coach can suggest activities based on your profile and preferences. Before registering you, it asks for your approval. You should see what activity, date, time, and location are being proposed before you agree.

If you change your mind, do not approve the sign-up. If the activity details look wrong, ask the coach to search again or choose another option.

## Key points

- The coach gives general healthy-living information grounded in these guides.
- It is not a doctor and does not diagnose.
- For urgent symptoms, call 995.
- Use HealthHub for personal health records.
- Healthpoints are the Healthy 365 rewards currency, and eVouchers are merchant vouchers redeemed with Healthpoints.
- The coach asks for approval before registering you for an activity.

## When to talk to a health professional

Speak to a doctor or pharmacist for personal medical questions, treatment decisions, symptoms, or advice that depends on your own health history. Use emergency services when symptoms are urgent.

_This guide is sample content written for the LiveWell Coach workshop. It is general information, not medical advice. If you have symptoms or a health condition, speak to a doctor._
